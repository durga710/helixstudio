Helix Space v2 — Classrooms, Assignments, Plans

Three additions to Spaces. Each works the same way for everyone — create a Space, share one invite link, and the rest happens in the editor you already use.
🎓
1 · CLASSROOM
SPACES
A Space can now be a team or a classroom
A team works like before — everyone shares projects with everyone. A classroom adds one role: the person who created it (the instructor) can post assignments and open each student's assignment work read-only. Students still own their workspaces, and classmates can't see each other's assignment work.
How it worksOne field on the Space (kind: team | classroom) switches the behavior. Access is checked on the server for every file read: owner → full access; Space member → shared projects only; classroom instructor → submissions linked to their assignments, read-only.
📋
2 · ASSIGNMENTS
Hand out starter code, collect work, review with AI help
InstructorCreates an assignment — instructions, due date, optional starter project
→
Student"Start" copies the starter into their own workspace — full editor + AI
→
StudentSubmits when done (can keep working until it's graded)
→
InstructorOpens the work read-only, runs an AI review, writes feedback + a grade
→
StudentSees feedback and grade once it's marked reviewed
How it worksEach submission is one database row tying student ↔ assignment ↔ their copied workspace. The copy is independent (starting from a 400-file-max snapshot of the starter). The AI review is a single model call with the instructor's configured model; its output stays instructor-only.
💺
3 · PLANS &
SEATS
Free plan for small groups, seats for bigger ones
Every Space starts free: up to 5 members and 2 assignments. A subscription adds seats (one per member) and removes the assignment limit — classrooms automatically get the education rate. If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted and nobody is removed; the Space just stops accepting new members past the free limit.
How it worksCheckout and billing management happen on Stripe's hosted pages; Helix stores only the plan, seat count, and renewal date on the Space. A signature-verified webhook (/api/billing/webhook) keeps that in sync. All limits are enforced server-side on the join and assignment-creation routes.
Helix Studio · Space v2 · models: Space.kind/plan/seats · Assignment · AssignmentSubmission — routes: /api/spaces/[id]/assignments*, /api/spaces/[id]/billing/*, /api/billing/webhook
